1Hardware Tare

Remove all weight from the scale (empty platform). Click to zero the raw ADC output. Wait for Stable: Yes first.

2Set Calibration Point

Place a known reference weight on the scale. Wait for it to stabilize, enter the exact weight (kg), then click Set. Point must be 1 or 2.

3Save or Revert

Save persists the calibration to NVS (survives power cycle). Cancel clears the two-point cal and reverts to the previous single-point factor.
