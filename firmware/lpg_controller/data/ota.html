Firmware OTA Update

Upload a compiled .bin image to update the controller over Wi-Fi. Status auto-refreshes every 3 s.

Before You Upload

  • OTA is blocked during an active fill. Stop or complete the fill first.
  • Do not power off the device during upload — firmware corruption may result.
  • The controller restarts automatically after a successful update.
  • Upload only a .bin built for KC868-A6 (ESP32, 4 MB flash, custom partition table).
  • Manufacturing PIN is required. It is printed on the unit label.
